 Adobo is one of the oldest dishes in Philippine cuisine, with roots that reach back before the arrival of the Spanish colonizers The name comes from the Spanish word "adobar," meaning to marinate or season, but Filipinos were already cooking meat in vinegar long before that name arrived Filipino cooks have passed down recipes like Adobo through generations, adjusting vinegar, soy sauce, and garlic to their family's taste.
