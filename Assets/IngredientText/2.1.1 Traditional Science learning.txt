2.1.1 Traditional Science learning in local recipes: A cross-disciplinary exposition 
This research endeavors to conduct a scientific exploration of Filipino traditional cuisines, specifically by identifying and analyzing the underlying scientific principles inherent in their preparation and composition. This study concurrently seeks to articulate the profound significance and multidisciplinary contributions of these local dishes, particularly to the economic strategies of Local Government Units (LGUs) and the knowledge base of the academic community. Recognizing the substantial potential of these culinary traditions to bolster livelihood programs, enrich educational curricula, support the One Town, One Product (OTOP) initiative, and ensure the preservation of cultural identity, this paper ultimately advocates for robust, cross-sectoral collaboration among municipal stakeholders to collectively promote, enhance, and strategically develop local culinary assets.
	The Philippine Statistics Authority estimates that 2.3 million Filipinos emigrated in 2018 alone [6], carrying cultural practices, including adobo preparation, to host countries worldwide. This diaspora has generated regional adaptations while simultaneously reinforcing adobo's status as a symbol of Filipino identity abroad. The dish exemplifies culinary syncretism: an indigenous preservation technique, a Spanish-imposed name, and a Chinese-influenced condiment (soy sauce) combined into a preparation that remains distinctly Filipino.


2.1.2 Exploring Culinary Heritage: Insights from Local Gatekeepers in the Province of Antique Philippines 
Food is a powerful expression of personal identity and cultural heritage, shaped by a rich tapestry of history, aesthetics, social customs, and symbolic meanings. Gaining insight into these dimensions helps us understand how cuisine reflects the collective memory and values of a community. In the Philippines, food is more than nourishment—it is a reflection of national character and cultural consciousness. Filipino cuisine offers a window into how people see themselves, their roles within society, and their place in the world. Within households, meals often serve as a bridge to ancestral traditions, preserving stories and practices passed down through generations. In regions like the province of Antique, traditional dishes proudly showcase the unique heritage and cultural identity of the local community.


2.1.3  Filipino Cuisine: A Rich Tapestry Of Culinary Tradition  
Filipino cuisine offers a vibrant and multifaceted culinary experience, masterfully blending bold innovation with familiar comfort. Rooted in centuries of indigenous practices and enriched by a tapestry of foreign influences, it showcases a dynamic fusion of primary flavors—most notably the interplay of sweet, sour, and salty, with occasional accents of spice or bitterness. This rich gastronomic heritage recently gained timely recognition with the government’s 2018 declaration of April as Filipino Food Month. As H.E. Ambassador Millicent Cruz Paredes reflected, Filipino food profoundly embodies the nation’s enduring culture of hospitality, where the act of sharing meals is central to both everyday life and festive occasions. Ambassador Paredes also highlighted the tradition of kamayan—or eating with one’s hands—as a deeply symbolic practice that powerfully fosters unity and a sense of community around the dining table.

2.1.4  Pinoy on the Plate: Uniting the World Through the Exquisite Taste of Filipino Cuisine
Filipino cuisine stands as a flavorful testament to the nation's rich and multifaceted cultural heritage, having been distinctively shaped by a blend of indigenous, Spanish, Chinese, and American influences. As the Philippines intensifies its engagement with the global community, its culinary traditions have naturally emerged as powerful tools of cultural diplomacy, effectively fostering mutual understanding and dialogue across international borders. This discussion explores the process by which Filipino food, rooted in a deep historical narrative, is currently gaining international recognition while simultaneously confronting challenges in achieving broader global visibility. Through this lens, the evolving international role of Filipino cuisine becomes a compelling demonstration of the Philippines' cultural identity and its increasingly important position in an interconnected world.
